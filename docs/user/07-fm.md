# FM

FM receive and transmit are verified on the DM-1701, with CTCSS and DCS tones at 12.5 and 25 kHz.

## Quick settings on an FM channel

Press **\*** on Home. The tiles are laid out like the keypad: the number key acts on the tile directly.

<img src="../ux/screens/160x120i5/quick_fm.png" width="200" alt="Quick settings on an FM channel">

| Key | Tile | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Power | Each press: low (50 mW), the default (1 W), high (5 W) |
| **2** | Squelch | Opens the squelch editor |
| **3** | Tone | Opens the tone editor |
| **4** | Mode | Listen and talk in DMR on the same frequencies, for now |
| **5** | Monitor | Latches the squelch open, or closes it again |
| **6** | VOX | Voice-operated transmit on or off (optional build) |
| **7** | Scan | Scan on or off |
| **8** | Width | Bandwidth 12.5 or 25 kHz (in the VFO: Step) |
| **9** | Bright | Opens the brightness editor |
| **0** | | Opens Settings |

**Up** and **Down** move between the tiles, **OK** acts on the focused one. Press **\*** or **Back** to close. Every
change is temporary; hold **OK** to save it to the channel.

The top side key also steps the power, one level per press through all nine levels (50 mW to 5 W).

## Squelch

1. **\***, **2**. The squelch editor opens.
2. Press **Up** or **Down**, or a number key from **0** to **9**. The change is live: you hear it.
3. Press **OK**, **\*** or **Back** to close.

The squelch level shows on Home as "SQ" and a number. The radio-wide default is in **Settings > Talk & listen >
Default squelch** (5 unless changed).

<img src="../ux/screens/160x120i5/quick_fm_squelch.png" width="200" alt="Editing the squelch">

## Monitor

- Hold **SK1** to open the squelch while you hold it. The status line shows MON.
- **\***, **5** latches the monitor on until you turn it off with **\***, **5** again.

## Tones (CTCSS and DCS)

1. **\***, **3**. The tone editor opens on the transmit tone. The radio shows "Up/Dn tone  # TX/RX".
2. Press **Up** or **Down** to step through the CTCSS tones and DCS codes. The change is live.
3. Press **#** to choose which tone you edit: both receive and transmit (the start when they are the same), transmit
   only, or receive only.
4. Press **OK** or **\*** to close.

Home shows the tone as a chip, for example "T100.0". "T" marks a transmit tone that differs from the receive tone,
"R" a receive tone without a transmit tone, "No tone" none.

## Bandwidth

**\***, **8** switches between 12.5 and 25 kHz. Home shows "12.5k" or "25k".

## Mode

**\***, **4** switches an FM channel to DMR on the same frequencies, for now ("DMR for now"). The timeslot is the
channel's, else 1. **Back** on Home switches back. Mode isn't changed in the VFO.

## VOX

VOX is an optional build (`CONFIG_VOX=y`). In a default build the VOX tile is dimmed. When it is built, **\***, **6**
turns it on for FM channels ("VOX on: speak to transmit"). The radio transmits when you speak and stops 1.5 s after
you stop. Received audio never keys it, and **PTT** works as usual. Not tested on the radio yet.

## Busy channel lockout, roger beep, tone burst

The radio core has busy channel lockout, a roger beep and a 1750 Hz tone burst for repeater access. There is no menu
item or key for them yet. Developers set them over the debug link.

## Transmit timeout

The radio stops transmitting after the transmit timeout and shows "TX time-out reached". A channel's own timeout from
the codeplug wins; when it is 0, **Settings > Talk & listen > TX timeout** applies (3 minutes unless changed). A
warning beep sounds 10 s before the timeout.
